Sintel (2010) — Time-Aligned Sub-Beat Grounding Reference:

Prologue.A — Black Screen & Opening Credits Over Empty Snowy Mountains (00:00 - 00:25):
The film opens on a black screen (00:00 - 00:03) before gliding aerially through an empty, misty snow-covered mountain pass displaying production credit titles. No characters appear in this window.

Act I.A1 — Sintel's Snow Trek and the Gatekeeper's Leap (00:25 - 00:47):
Sintel trudges alone through deep snow carrying a wooden staff. Pausing in the freezing wind, she spots the unmasked, bald, bearded Gatekeeper (with tall wooden banner poles strapped to his back) leaping down from a snowy ridge through the mist to ambush her. They clash weapons and Sintel is knocked backward down the slope.

Act I.A2 — The Staff Breaks & Sintel Is Pinned (00:47 - 01:05):
Sintel and the Gatekeeper trade strikes in the snow until the Gatekeeper's curved spear-blade slices Sintel's wooden staff cleanly in two (~00:55). Disarmed, Sintel is thrown onto her back in the snow as the Gatekeeper looms over her and raises his spear-blade to impale her. (The fight does NOT end in this window).

Act I.A3 — Fatal Counter-Strike, Claiming the Spear-Blade, and Snowstorm Collapse (01:05 - 01:41):
As the Gatekeeper presses his spear-blade down toward Sintel's chest, she holds the shaft back with one hand, grabs the sharp broken half of her wooden staff from the snow, and drives it upward into the Gatekeeper (~01:10). He collapses dead in the snow, leaving his spear-blade resting across her chest. Sintel recovers, pulls the Gatekeeper's curved spear-blade from the snow beside his body (~01:26), trudges past the 'SINTEL' title emblem, and collapses exhausted in the snowstorm (~01:38).

Act I.B — Awakening in the Shaman's Hut (01:41 - 02:35):
Sintel awakens inside a firelit desert hut tended by an elderly, Shaman. The Shaman examines her captured spear-blade by the fire, notes its dark past, hands Sintel a bowl of soup, and asks what brings her to the land of the gatekeepers. Sintel reveals she is searching for a dragon.

Act II.A — Flashback in Ishtar: Scavenging & Finding Injured Scales (02:35 - 03:35):
In a flashback to the sunlit desert city of Ishtar, a stocky townsman walks down an alley with a cart while teenage Sintel hides behind crates (02:35 - 02:42). Sintel sneaks out to scavenge a piece of fruit, hears a sound, and climbs a ladder onto a tiled rooftop (~02:58) where she discovers Scales, a small baby red dragon with an injured wing. She coaxes Scales with the fruit and brings him to her shelter.

Act II.B — Healing Scales & Learning to Fly in Ishtar (03:35 - 04:55):
Sintel bandages Scales's injured wing, feeds him, and forms a close bond with him over time in Ishtar. Once his wing heals, Sintel removes the bandage and helps Scales practice flying across the city streets and rooftops.

Act II.C — The Kidnapping of Scales Over Ishtar (04:55 - 05:35):
As Scales flies high above the rooftops of Ishtar, a giant wild Adult Dragon swoops down from the clouds, snatches baby Scales in its talons, and carries him away despite Sintel's desperate chase across the rooftops.

Act III.A — The Cross-Continental Quest Montage (05:35 - 07:05):
Determined to rescue Scales, Sintel journeys alone across bamboo forests, scorching deserts, and snowy mountains over years of searching until collapsing in the snowstorm.

Act III.B — The Spear-Blade Glyph Reveal in the Shaman's Hut (07:05 - 08:15):
Back in the Shaman's hut, Sintel despairs that she has failed, but the Shaman points out the tree emblem engraved on the Gatekeeper's spear-blade—revealing that the Gatekeeper guards the dragon's volcanic peak nearby. Sintel seizes the spear-blade and rushes out.

Act III.C — Climbing the Volcanic Peak & Entering the Dragon Cavern (08:15 - 09:45):
Sintel climbs a jagged volcanic peak and creeps into a dark cavern lair, where she spots a fledgling baby red dragon curled beside a giant sleeping Adult Dragon. Mistaking the hatchling for Scales, she tries to coax it away quietly.

Act IV.A — The Cavern Battle & The Fatal Hesitation (09:45 - 11:15):
The giant cavern Adult Dragon awakens and attacks furiously. It knocks Sintel's spear-blade aside, pins her to the stone floor, and prepares to strike, but pauses as it recognizes Sintel's eyes and pulls back. Unaware of why the dragon hesitated, Sintel grabs her spear-blade and plunges it into the Adult Dragon's chest.

Act IV.B — The Wing-Scar & Blood-Reflection Twist and Cavern Collapse (11:15 - 12:35):
As the dying cavern Adult Dragon collapses, Sintel sees the familiar healed notch scar on its wing and glimpses her own aged, gray-streaked reflection in a pool of the dragon's blood—realizing decades have passed and the cavern Adult Dragon she just killed is actually her grown-up Scales (not the wild dragon that snatched him years ago), while the fledgling hatchling is Scales's own offspring. As the cavern begins to collapse around them, a grief-stricken Sintel flees into the falling snow while the orphaned baby dragon trails behind her.

Epilogue — Closing Credits (12:35 - 14:48):
Closing credits roll over dark backgrounds and concept art.
