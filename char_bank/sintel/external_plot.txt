Sintel (2010) — Full Plot & Ending Twist Synopsis:

Act I — The Snowstorm and the Shaman's Hut (00:00 - 03:35):
Sintel, a lone young woman warrior bundled in a fur-lined cloak and carrying a wooden staff, wanders across a desolate, snow-swept mountain wilderness searching for someone. She is ambushed by a masked gatekeeper warrior, whom she defeats after a tense close-quarters fight, claiming his distinctive spear-blade before collapsing in the freezing storm. She awakens inside a warmly lit desert hut tended by an elderly tribal Shaman with white dreadlocks, who offers her shelter and asks what brings her to the land of the gatekeepers. Sintel reveals that she is searching the world for a dragon companion who was taken from her.

Act II — Flashback in Ishtar and the Kidnapping of Scales (03:35 - 05:15):
In a flashback to Sintel's earlier life in the bustling sunlit city of Ishtar, she scavenges for food and comes across a wounded, frightened baby red dragon with an injured wing. She brings the hatchling back to her rooftop shelter, feeds him, wraps a makeshift bandage around his torn wing, and names him Scales. Over time, Sintel and Scales form a deep affectionate bond as she heals him and teaches him how to fly again. One day, as Scales soars above the rooftops of Ishtar, a giant winged Adult Dragon descends out of the clouds, snatches Scales in its talons, and carries him away into the sky despite Sintel's desperate chase.

Act III — The Long Quest and the Mountain Lair (05:15 - 10:15):
Vowing to bring Scales back, Sintel sets out on an arduous cross-continental quest across icy mountain ridges, misty bamboo forests, and scorching dunes—growing visibly older and more weathered over years of fruitless searching before collapsing in the desert and waking up in the Shaman's hut. Encouraged by the Shaman's warning that some things cannot be found the way we expect, Sintel climbs a jagged volcanic peak and enters a massive cavern lair. Deep inside the cave, she spots a giant sleeping Adult Dragon curled beside a small baby red dragon. Hoping to rescue the young dragon quietly, she creeps toward it.

Act IV — The Battle and the Wing-Scar Twist (10:15 - 14:48):
Before Sintel can escape with the hatchling, the giant Adult Dragon wakes up and attacks furiously to protect the cavern. In the brutal clash, the Adult Dragon pins Sintel helplessly to the cavern floor and prepares a fatal strike, but abruptly pauses as it stares closely into Sintel's eyes—recognizing her and pulling back instead of killing her. Unaware of why the beast hesitated, Sintel seizes the opening and drives her spear straight into the Adult Dragon's chest. As the giant dragon collapses dying on the stone floor (~11:15), Sintel notices a familiar healed notch and torn bandage-wrap scar along its wing—the exact wound she bound on Scales years ago. Realizing too late that far more years had passed than she knew, the giant Adult Dragon she has just slain was actually her grown-up Scales, and the little red dragon cowering nearby is Scales's own offspring. Shattered by grief, Sintel drops her staff and walks blindly out into the falling snow while the orphaned baby dragon quietly trails after her.
