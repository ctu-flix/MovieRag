Sintel (2010) — Full Plot & Ending Twist Synopsis:

Act I.A — The Snowstorm and the Gatekeeper Ambush (00:00 - 02:05):
Sintel, a lone young woman warrior bundled in a fur-lined cloak and carrying a wooden staff, wanders across a desolate, snow-swept mountain wilderness. At a mountain pass, she is ambushed by a masked Gatekeeper warrior wielding a curved spear-blade with a circular hilt ring. After a tense close-quarters fight in the snow, Sintel disarms and defeats the Gatekeeper, claims his distinctive curved spear-blade, and collapses in the freezing storm.

Act I.B — Awakening in the Shaman's Hut (02:05 - 03:35):
Sintel awakens inside a warmly lit desert hut tended by an elderly tribal Shaman with long white dreadlocks and ceremonial robes, who offers her shelter and asks what brings her to the land of the gatekeepers. Sintel reveals that she is searching the world for a dragon companion who was taken from her.

Act II.A — Flashback in Ishtar: Finding and Healing Scales (03:35 - 04:35):
In a flashback to Sintel's earlier life in the bustling sunlit city of Ishtar, she scavenges for food and comes across a wounded, frightened baby red dragon with an injured wing. She brings the hatchling back to her rooftop shelter, feeds him, wraps a makeshift bandage around his torn wing, and names him Scales. Over time, Sintel and Scales form a deep affectionate bond as she heals him and teaches him to fly again.

Act II.B — The Kidnapping of Scales Over Ishtar (04:35 - 05:15):
As Scales practices flying and soars high above the rooftops of Ishtar, a giant winged Adult Dragon descends out of the clouds, snatches Scales in its talons, and carries him away into the sky despite Sintel's desperate chase across the city.

Act III.A — The Cross-Continental Quest and Snowstorm Collapse (05:15 - 07:15):
Vowing to bring Scales back, Sintel sets out on an arduous solitary quest across scorching desert dunes, misty bamboo forests, and rocky mountain passes—growing visibly older and more weathered over years of fruitless searching before collapsing exhausted in a blinding snowstorm.

Act III.B — Return to the Shaman's Hut and Renewed Resolve (07:15 - 08:40):
Back inside the Shaman's hut at the end of her flashback, the Shaman warns Sintel that she has reached the end of her search and that some things cannot be found the way we expect. Hearing wingbeats or wind outside, Sintel grabs her cloak and the Gatekeeper's spear-blade and rushes back out into the night.

Act III.C — Climbing the Volcanic Peak and Entering the Dragon Cavern (08:40 - 10:15):
Carrying the Gatekeeper's curved spear-blade with its circular hilt ring, Sintel climbs a jagged volcanic peak at night and enters a massive dark cavern lair. Deep inside the cave, she discovers a fledgling baby red dragon curled beside a giant sleeping Adult Dragon and creeps forward hoping to rescue the hatchling quietly.

Act IV — The Cavern Battle and the Wing-Scar Twist (10:15 - 12:24):
Before Sintel can escape with the hatchling, the giant Adult Dragon wakes up and attacks furiously to protect the cavern. In the brutal clash, the Adult Dragon knocks Sintel's weapon aside, pins her helplessly to the cavern floor, and prepares a fatal strike, but abruptly pauses as it stares closely into Sintel's eyes—recognizing her and pulling back instead of killing her. Unaware of why the beast hesitated, Sintel seizes her spear-blade and drives it straight into the Adult Dragon's chest. As the giant dragon collapses dying on the stone floor (~11:15), Sintel notices a familiar healed notch and bandage-wrap scar along its wing—the exact wound she bound on Scales years ago. Realizing too late that the giant Adult Dragon she has just slain was her grown-up Scales and the little red dragon cowering nearby is Scales's offspring, a grief-stricken Sintel walks blindly out into the falling snow while the orphaned baby dragon quietly trails after her.
